# Ledger

**A story-driven custom trader with a 40+ quest storyline built around PMC dogtags, names, and the people who owned them.**

Ledger is a quiet, methodical trader with a habit of keeping records.

His requests begin simply enough. Dogtags, factions, levels, occasional specific requirements. As time goes on, those requests become more varied, and like many things in life, the more time you invest in something, the more you begin to realise there may be more to it than first appeared.

Ledger is not really a shop.

He does sell a small selection of useful items, and he will buy from you, but his stock is deliberately limited. Think of it less as a trader inventory and more as whatever useful equipment happens to be lying around in the room. His purpose is the questline, not retail.

His loyalty is earned **only through his own quests** and is completely independent of your PMC level. That means Ledger works just as well on a brand-new profile as he does when added to an established or high-level character.

The only way to progress with him is to do the work.

## Companion to Realistic PMC Names

Ledger was created as a natural companion to **Realistic PMC Names**.

The two mods are completely independent and neither requires the other, but they complement each other extremely well. Realistic PMC Names makes PMCs feel more like actual players and less like disposable AI. Ledger gives those names and dogtags a little more weight.

There are no hidden dependencies, scripted encounters, or required cross-mod events. They simply work well together.

## What Ledger Includes

- A completely custom trader
- Original portrait, dialogue and personality
- Independent loyalty progression
- A deliberately small specialist shop
- A 40+ quest storyline
- Progression that works on both fresh and existing profiles

That is really all you need to know.

## Recommended Setup

Ledger can be played perfectly well on its own, but if immersion is your priority, I strongly recommend using it alongside:

- **Realistic PMC Names**
- **SAIN**
- **Acid's Progressive Bot System**
- **Orbit**

Together, these mods can transform the feel of SPT.

SAIN gives PMCs much more convincing combat behaviour. Progressive Bot System makes their progression feel more natural. Orbit helps them move through the maps more like actual players instead of simply occupying familiar scav locations. Realistic PMC Names gives them believable identities.

Add Ledger on top of that, and PMC encounters start to feel much less disposable.

Huge credit to the authors of SAIN, Progressive Bot System and Orbit. They are genuinely transformative mods and a major part of what makes heavily-modded SPT feel so alive.

## How to Play Ledger

Ledger works especially well if you enjoy PMC hunting, but he does not demand that you build your entire playthrough around him.

You can focus on his quests deliberately, or simply let them run alongside your normal progression as you collect tags and return to him over time.

Some tasks are easy.

Some are not.

Some rewards may seem underwhelming at first.

Stick with it.

And if Ledger asks you about someone in particular, pay attention.
